संवाद — यानी बातचीत।

“Samvaad” means dialogue. Design Samvaad is a community-run gathering by GDG Noida where product designers, industry leaders, students, and makers meet in real rooms — an office, a campus, a studio — to swap process, critique work, and push the craft forward.

It’s for the curious: the portfolio-builder, the career-switcher, the seasoned lead. No stage-and-audience distance — just designers in conversation. That’s why it matters: careers are built in these rooms, and friendships too.
